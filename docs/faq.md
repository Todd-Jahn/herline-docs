# Frequently Asked Questions

## Product

### What is Herline?

Herline is an AI workspace for deep reading, course and presentation creation, research, distribution, and voice rehearsal. AI accelerates execution while people retain judgment and responsibility.

### Who is it for?

Herline has separate paths for youth learners and guardians, adult professionals, youth educators, and invited operators or partners. The live product determines which path and capabilities an account can access.

### How is it different from a general chatbot?

Herline organizes work around persistent product surfaces, structured artifacts, versioned workflows, exports, and review. It is still AI software: output can be incomplete or wrong and requires human verification.

### What should a new user do first?

Start from the current [herline.vip](https://herline.vip) entry that matches your role. Adult users can begin with deep reading or voice rehearsal; youth and educator paths use their corresponding product entry.

### Is every capability available to every account?

No. Availability depends on plan, role, program, region, rollout, and product safety requirements. A capability appearing in documentation does not override the live account entitlement.

### Where are current prices and quotas?

Use [herline.vip](https://herline.vip). This repository does not duplicate prices or quotas because they change more frequently than public documentation.

## Product concepts

### Are Atlas, Helena, and D2B the same thing?

No. Atlas is a user-facing planning surface, Helena is the Agent that powers its conversation, and D2B is a deep-reading workflow.

### Are Studio, Holly, and B2C the same thing?

No. Studio is the course-planning surface, Holly is the course-planning Agent, and B2C is the course-generation workflow.

### What is Assistant / Harmon?

Assistant is the product surface; Harmon is the voice-rehearsal Agent. It supports practice and feedback but is not a therapist, crisis service, legal adviser, or real-world negotiation agent.

### What is Hypatia?

Hypatia is a Pro research surface and Agent label backed by an evidence-oriented workflow. Released capabilities include versioned projects and eligible reruns or resumption. Specific report types and downstream handoffs may have separate availability.

## Data, privacy, and safety

### Is my content public by default?

No. Sharing requires an explicit user action. Anyone with an active share link may be able to view the material shown on that page, so users should review the content and recipient before sharing.

### Does Herline train models on private data?

The current privacy and AI-content policies state that raw private content, raw voice, and identifiable personal information are not directly placed into shared or general-purpose training sets. Other quality, safety, and analytics processing is governed by purpose, authorization, de-identification status, and applicable law. Read the live [Privacy Policy](https://herline.vip/legal/privacy) for the authoritative terms.

### What additional rules apply to youth users?

Users under 18 require guardian authorization. Voice, sensitive information, public display, and other higher-risk processing can require additional notice or consent. A school, community, or program organizer does not replace the legal guardian where guardian authorization is required.

### Can I rely on AI output for high-impact decisions?

No. Users must review output before publication, teaching delivery, commercial use, or decisions with legal, financial, health, education, employment, or personal-safety consequences.

### How do I request access, correction, deletion, or account cancellation?

Use the product’s privacy/account controls or contact `legal@herline.vip` / `customer-support@herline.vip`. Retention exceptions required for transactions, security, disputes, consent records, or law are explained in the live policies.

## Technology and integration

### Is Herline open source?

No. Herline’s core product and engines are proprietary. This repository publishes documentation and examples under CC BY 4.0; that license does not apply to the product source code.

### Is there a public API or MCP integration?

Only interfaces explicitly published in the live developer area should be treated as available. Internal, experimental, or validation-stage integrations are not public commitments.

### Which languages and formats are supported?

Chinese and English are the primary product languages. Import/export formats vary by surface and account; the live product shows the supported options for a specific workflow.

### What happens when a long task fails?

The product distinguishes completed, failed, interrupted, and resumable work. Some released workflows support versioned reruns or eligible resumption. The interface for the specific task shows the available recovery action.
