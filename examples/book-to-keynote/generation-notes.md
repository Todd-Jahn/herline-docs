# Editorial Notes and Evidence Boundary

This sample demonstrates an artifact structure, not a measured product result.

## How it was assembled

The example combines:

1. a synthetic speaker profile and audience brief;
2. selected ideas associated with the named source;
3. an AI-assisted keynote draft; and
4. human editing for structure, tone, examples, and stage cues.

The repository does not provide preserved run logs, release-state evidence, or a controlled comparison that would support claims about generation speed, edit ratio, quality improvement, or time saved. Earlier timing and percentage estimates have therefore been removed.

## What requires human review

- **Source accuracy:** verify every quotation, study description, number, and attribution against an authorized source.
- **Personal voice:** replace synthetic anecdotes with the speaker's own authorized experience.
- **Product claims:** do not reuse fictional metrics as real business evidence.
- **Audience fit:** adjust length, terminology, examples, and stage directions for the actual setting.
- **Rights and privacy:** confirm permission to use source material and personal or organizational information.

## Availability boundary

The sequence shown in this example is a conceptual connected workflow. Specific surfaces, export formats, automation steps, and entitlements may vary by role, account, and region. Check the live product for current access.

## What this example is not

- a customer case study;
- a production benchmark;
- a promise that a first draft is publication-ready;
- evidence that every named workflow is enabled for every user; or
- a substitute for factual, legal, rights, or editorial review.
